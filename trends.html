2026 Forecast Inferences

Based on 10-year trend analysis and cost inflation modeling

Data sources & methodology: Historical benchmark data compiled from WordStream annual benchmark reports (2016–2025), Triple Whale industry reports (2022–2025), Revealbot Meta benchmarks (2020–2025), and Pixis advertising intelligence reports. 2026 projections are calculated using linear regression on observed year-over-year trends, adjusted for known cost inflation factors including increased platform competition, iOS 14+ privacy changes (2021), cookie deprecation signals, and AI-driven bidding adoption. Projections represent median estimates — actual performance may vary based on account maturity, creative quality, targeting precision, and market conditions. Data reflects US market averages. Note on Health & Wellness CTR: Some 2025 DTC-specific sources (e.g. Triple Whale supplement brand data) report CTR as low as 1.49% for a narrow segment of health accounts. Pietro IQ uses normalized WordStream Health & Medical category data (2.55%) as the more representative benchmark for the broader Health & Wellness vertical. Both figures are valid depending on your specific sub-category.
